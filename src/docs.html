openplayx-asr

Asynchronous speech recognition for OpenPlayX (whisper.cpp, in-process). Upload 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV; times in results are audio_ms. All /v1 endpoints need Authorization: Bearer <token>. Full contract: openapi.yaml (OpenAPI 3.1).

EndpointPurpose
GET /v1/capabilitiesModels, audio format, limits
POST /v1/transcription-jobs?model=&language=Upload audio (headers Idempotency-Key, X-Content-SHA256) → 202 + job
GET /v1/transcription-jobs?idempotency_key=Find a job after a lost response
GET /v1/transcription-jobs/{id}Status and progress
GET /v1/transcription-jobs/{id}/resultTranscript (409 until succeeded)
POST /v1/transcription-jobs/{id}/cancelIdempotent cancel
DELETE /v1/transcription-jobs/{id}Delete a finished job and its data
